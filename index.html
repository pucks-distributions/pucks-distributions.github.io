about program

Hello! Pucks is a space for exhibition making in my home and an ongoing subscription-like publication – possibly in your home. Each exhibition at Pucks is accompanied by a set of printed pages, though at times, pages may also be produced independently of an exhibition. These are sent by post to everyone who owns a publication. The exhibition program is grounded in a set of limitations. Pucks runs an exclusively international, non-local program without any transportation of artworks or people travelling. All shows are produced on site in absence of the artists. Sometimes by me alone, sometimes with friends and colleagues. These limitations stem from my own interests in logistics and systems of distribution but are as much indebted to the spatial limitations of my home and a desire to escape from certain institutional norms and procedures. It is an attempt to evoke the world-part of the artworld through a non-cargo based logistics of ideas, conversations, outsourcing, interpretation and collaboration. The publication consists of an edition of 180 pieces which, when placed side by side, cover exactly the entire floor of the exhibition space. I like to think of these as a kind of distributed second floor. Another etage, that continues the real space of my flat into the homes of other people.

Pucks takes its name from English mythology, in which Puck is a merry household fairy known for mischievous pranks and practical jokes. At night, Puck may also perform small services for the household he inhabits. Currently, Pucks is located at Schmalzhofgasse 18/2/27, 1060 Vienna but might move elsewhere as life goes on. If you are interested in a publication, it is published by fug(ue–a–e) or you send me a DM via instagram or write me a mail. Following are some images of it.
